Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy explains what information Flash Analyst handles, why, and how you can see, change or delete it. Flash Analyst is run by Flash Analyst Pty Ltd (ABN 65 703 332 772) in New South Wales, Australia, owned by Alex C. Contact us at flashanalyst@gmail.com.

In short: your company's numbers never leave your device. We don't use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking. We only collect what we need to sell you a license and support you.

Your Financial Data Stays On Your Device

The Flash Analyst app on this website, the free score calculator and the downloadable program all work on your own device. The company figures you enter, the files you add and the reports it makes are not sent to us or anyone else.

What We Collect

  • When you buy: your name, email address and order details (edition, date and order number). Our payment provider, Lemon Squeezy, handles your card details; we never see them.
  • When you activate a license key: your key and a short device label (like "Browser on MacIntel") are sent to Lemon Squeezy to check the key, and again about once a week. Nothing else is sent.
  • When you contact us: your contact details and whatever you write to us.

We don't ask for anything else, and no form on this site collects personal information.

What Is Saved On Your Device

This site saves a few small settings in your browser (called local storage), such as dark mode and, if you use the app, your license details. They stay on your device and are never sent to us. See the cookie policy for the full list.

How We Use It

  • To deliver your purchase and check your license.
  • To keep a record of licenses, so we can confirm your license or help if you lose your key.
  • To reply to your questions and tell you about important changes to your purchase.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Emails

We only send emails about your purchase, such as your receipt and license key. We don't send marketing emails. If we ever start, we will only send them to people who ask to receive them, and every one will have an unsubscribe link that works straight away.

Who Else Handles Data

  • Lemon Squeezy processes payments, sends receipts and license keys, and checks license keys. Its own privacy policy applies to the checkout.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may log visitors' IP addresses for security.

That's all. The website's fonts and the app's engine are stored on this website itself, so loading a page doesn't contact anyone else. There are no analytics, advertising or social media tools on this site.

Children

Flash Analyst is made for businesses, and you must be 18 or older to buy it (or have a parent or guardian buy it for you). We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you think a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

How Long We Keep It

We keep license records while your license is valid, so we can support you. Purchase records may need to be kept for longer where the law requires it, for example tax records, which in Australia are generally kept for five years.

See, Correct Or Delete Your Information

You can ask us at any time to show you, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Contact us and say what you'd like. We'll reply within 30 days, and we'll ask Lemon Squeezy to delete your order details too, except anything the law requires us to keep. Email flashanalyst@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy request".

To remove what's saved in your browser, use the button above. To free up your license key on a device, use "Remove from this device" in the app.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we handled your information, contact us first. If we don't fix it, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

Changes

We may update this policy. The version on this page, with its date, is the current one.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This policy explains what personal information Flash Analyst collects and how it is used.

Your financial data stays on your computer

The Flash Analyst software runs on your own computer. The company figures you enter, and the reports it creates, are not sent to us or anyone else.

To check your license, the software sends your license key and your computer's name to Lemon Squeezy when you activate it, and about once a week after that. Nothing else is sent.

What we collect

  • When you buy: your name, email address and purchase details (edition, date and license ID). Payment card details are handled by our payment provider, Lemon Squeezy, and we never see them.
  • When you email us: your email address and whatever you write.

How we use it

  • To deliver your purchase and create your personal licensed copy.
  • To keep a record of licenses, so we can confirm your license or replace a lost copy.
  • To reply to your questions and send you important updates about your purchase.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who else handles data

  • Lemon Squeezy processes payments and may handle sales tax. Its own privacy policy applies to the checkout.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may log visitors' IP addresses for security.
  • Google Fonts provides the website's fonts and may receive your IP address when the page loads.

How long we keep it

We keep license records for as long as your license is valid, so we can support you. You can ask us to delete your details at any time; we may need to keep basic purchase records where the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing georgesfc421@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy. The version on this page, with its date, is the current one.

Questions? Email georgesfc421@gmail.com.

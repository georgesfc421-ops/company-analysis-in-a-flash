How it works

Three steps, no retyping

  1. Send in the numbers

    Fill in the simple Excel template, or export the Profit & Loss and Balance Sheet straight from an accounting app.

  2. Flash Analyst reads them

    It finds revenue, costs, profit, assets, debt and cash flow on its own, even from messy exports with titles and indented accounts.

  3. Get the finished report

    A branded Excel workbook with your name on every page, ready to send to a client, a lender or a boss.

What's in the report

Four tabs that answer "how is this company doing?"

Tab 1 · Dashboard

The answer at a glance

Headline numbers, a 0–100 Financial Health Score with a letter grade, revenue and margin charts, key insights and watch-outs written in plain English.

Tab 2 · Financials

The numbers, cleaned up

Every year laid out as a proper financial statement, formatted and easy to read.

Tab 3 · Ratios

12 ratios as live formulas

Margins, growth, return on equity, current ratio, debt-to-equity, free cash flow and more. Change a number and everything updates.

Tab 4 · Forecast

Where it's heading

A 3-year projection with growth and margin assumptions you can adjust, plus a chart of actual against forecast.

Analyzing several companies? You also get a peer comparison workbook that ranks them side by side.

Getting the numbers in

Works with what people already have

Reads Excel and CSV files, with years across the top or down the side. It recognizes the report wording used by popular accounting apps:

  • QuickBooks
  • Xero
  • Wave
  • FreshBooks
  • Sage
  • Zoho Books
  • Excel
  • Google Sheets
  • Only revenue and net income are required. Every extra number adds more to the report.
  • Understands $1,234, 1.5M, (500) for losses, and names like "Sales" or "Net Profit".
  • Combines a Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow report into one analysis.

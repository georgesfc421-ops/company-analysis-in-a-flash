How it works

Three steps, no retyping

  1. 01

    Send in the numbers

    Fill in the simple Excel template, or export the Profit & Loss and Balance Sheet straight from an accounting app.

  2. 02

    Flash Analyst reads them

    It finds revenue, costs, profit, assets, debt and cash flow on its own, even from messy exports with titles and indented accounts.

  3. 03

    Get the finished report

    A branded Excel workbook with your name on every page, ready to send to a client, a lender or a boss.

Getting the numbers in

Works with what people already have

Reads Excel and CSV files, with years across the top or down the side. It recognizes the report wording used by popular accounting apps:

  • QuickBooks
  • Xero
  • Wave
  • FreshBooks
  • Sage
  • Zoho Books
  • Excel
  • Google Sheets
  • Only revenue and net income are required. Every extra number adds more to the report.
  • Understands $1,234, 1.5M, (500) for losses, and names like “Sales” or “Net Profit”.
  • Combines a Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow report into one analysis.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What do I need to run it?

A Windows or Mac computer with Python 3.8 or newer, plus one free add-on (openpyxl). You run one short command to make each report. It doesn't run on phones or tablets.

Do I have to retype my numbers?

No. Use the Excel template, or the Profit & Loss and Balance Sheet exports from your accounting app as Excel or CSV files.

Which accounting apps does it work with?

It recognizes the report wording used by QuickBooks, Xero, Wave, FreshBooks, Sage and Zoho Books, and reads Excel and Google Sheets files. Choose yearly columns when you export, not monthly.

Are my financial numbers uploaded anywhere?

No. Flash Analyst runs on your own computer, and your figures and reports stay there. See the privacy policy.

What's the difference between Basic and Pro?

Pro adds the Client Needs action plan tab to every report. Everything else is the same in both plans.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once, and all version 1 updates are included.
